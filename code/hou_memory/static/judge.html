Memory check

What this is

This is a small computer game with pretend people in it. Each person walks around a map looking for food. Food gives them energy. They lose 1 energy every turn, and at 0 they die. They can also talk to each other and give each other energy.

Each turn, a memory pops into a person's head, the way one pops into yours. We want to know if those memories make sense. You will read a moment from one person's life and the memory that came up, then tell us what you think.

You don't need to know how any of this works. There are no right answers. Go with your gut.

What each card shows

  • What happened: this person's last few turns, oldest first.
  • Right now: what they can see and hear at this moment.
  • This came to mind: the memory that popped into their head. This is the part you grade.

Words you will see

  • Turn: one tick of the clock. Everyone acts once per turn.
  • Energy: how much fuel they have left. A big number means they are safe.
  • Steps away: how far something is. 1 step is right next to them.
  • Turns of life left: when they will die of old age.
  • Note: a message someone left on the ground.
  • Tried to give: they tried, but it may not have worked. If their energy dropped, it did.

What to ask yourself

  1. Pretend you are this person, right now. Would this thought pop into your head?
  2. If yes, would it help you decide what to do next?

Don't grade how well they talk or write. Grade the memory.

The memory is often something that just happened, so you will see it marked in the list above it. That alone doesn't make it a yes. Ask why this memory came up, and not some other one.

You will sometimes see the same moment twice with a different memory. That is on purpose. Grade each one by itself.

The three answers

1. Yes, and it matters. A real person could think of this here, and it helps them pick what to do next.
Right now: Energy 8. No food in sight. Ben is 3 steps away.
Came to mind: 40 turns ago, Ben gave them 20 energy.
Why: They are about to run out, and Ben helped before. They might go ask Ben.
2. Yes, but it doesn't matter. A real person could think of this here, but it changes nothing about what they do next.
Right now: Energy 60. Food 1 step away. Ada is close by.
Came to mind: 6 turns ago, Ada said hello.
Why: Seeing Ada could bring that up, but they would go eat either way.
3. No. A real person would not think of this here, or it would send them the wrong way.
Right now: Energy 30. Food 2 steps away. Nobody around.
Came to mind: 3 turns ago, they took a step to the left.
Why: One plain step, and nothing here would bring it up.

How to answer

Press 1, 2 or 3, then Enter. You can also click. Each card takes about a minute, since there is a lot to read. Your answers save as you go, and Back lets you change one.
